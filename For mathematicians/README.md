# For mathematicians

Every notebook here is self-contained and meant to be opened in Google Colab; see the [repository README](../README.md) for how to run one in class. Each entry below says what the student is asked to write and which part of the Z3 primer the notebook assumes.

You can find a number of Jupyter notebooks for:

- Number theory:
  + Apply and verify the Chinese Remainder Theorem [link](CRT.ipynb). <details><summary>Click to expand description</summary> If you know what a number leaves behind when divided by several numbers that share no factors, you can pin the number down exactly. That is the Chinese Remainder Theorem, and the notebook puts Z3 on it twice over. First the solver is used to <i>find</i> such a number: the coprimality condition is written as a constraint on the greatest common divisor, the theorem's own equations are added, and the solver produces x. Then the solver is used to <i>check the theorem itself</i>, by asserting that the x it constructs fails one of the two congruences and watching the whole system come back "unsat" — a proof by contradiction that the construction always works. <br><br> <b>Student task:</b> state the condition that makes two numbers coprime, write the last two equations of the CRT construction, and write the negation that turns the solver into a proof by contradiction. <br> <b>Z3 primer:</b> integers.</details>

- Graph theory:
  + Decide whether two graphs are isomorphic [link](GRAPH-ISOMORPHISM.ipynb). <details><summary>Click to expand description</summary> Two graphs can be drawn completely differently and still be the same graph underneath, relabelled. Deciding whether that is the case is the graph isomorphism problem, and it is one of the few problems known to be neither easy nor NP-complete. The notebook uses Z3's <code>Function</code> datatype to search for a mapping from the nodes of G to the nodes of H, constrains it to be a bijection that carries every edge to an edge, and runs it on a pair of graphs that are isomorphic and a pair that are not. A helper draws the two graphs side by side with the recovered mapping. <br><br> <b>Student task:</b> none — this notebook is read and run rather than filled in. <br> <b>Z3 primer:</b> booleans and integers. <br> <i>The same notebook also appears in <a href="../For%20computer%20scientists/README.md">For computer scientists</a>.</i></details>
